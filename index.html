Why Builder-Owned

Rent from the builder,
not a landlord.

Every building on this site is purpose-built rental — designed, built, owned and managed by the developer. That changes what renting feels like.

No private landlord

You lease from the company that built the tower. No investor deciding to sell the unit, move a relative in, or hand you an N12.

Amenities in the lease

Gyms, pools, co-working, pet spas and lounges are part of the building — no condo maintenance fees, no separate membership.

Brand-new suites

Never lived in. Full-size appliances, in-suite laundry, quartz counters and real closets — specified by the builder, not a flipper.

On-site management

Concierge and a leasing team in the building. Maintenance goes through one office, not a text message to someone's cousin.

Real, published pricing

Every rent here is the asking rent from the building's own price list — not a discounted “effective” number that jumps at renewal.
