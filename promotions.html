The Fine Print, In Plain English

How these
actually work.

Free months are free months

The builder gives the months, on the lease term they set. It is not a lower monthly rent, which is why every price on this site is the full asking rent and not a discounted “effective” rent.

“Up to” means it varies

Where an offer says “up to”, the amount depends on the suite, the lease term and the move-in date. Ask us and we’ll confirm what applies to the specific suite you want.

Offers can end without notice

These are the builders’ promotions, not ours. They change, and they can be pulled or replaced at any time. Nothing here is confirmed until it is written into your lease.

Our side costs you nothing

We shortlist, book the tours and walk the paperwork through. You deal with one person instead of 7 leasing offices.
